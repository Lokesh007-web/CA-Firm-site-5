    Document Checklist

    • PAN and Aadhaar card
    • Form 16 / salary slips
    • Form 26AS / AIS statement
    • Bank account statements
    • Investment and deduction proofs
    • Capital gains statements, if applicable
    • PAN and Aadhaar of proprietor / partners / directors
    • Passport-size photograph
    • Business address proof (rent agreement / electricity bill)
    • Bank account details (cancelled cheque or statement)
    • Partnership deed / incorporation certificate, if applicable
    • PAN and Aadhaar of all directors / partners
    • Passport-size photographs
    • Address proof of directors (bank statement / utility bill)
    • Registered office proof with owner's NOC
    • Two or three proposed company / LLP names

    Ask a question
    What types of clients do you work with?

    We work with salaried individuals, professionals, freelancers, small and medium businesses, partnership firms, LLPs and companies.

    Can you help with GST registration and monthly filing?

    Yes. We handle new GST registration, monthly and quarterly return filing, reconciliation of purchase data and replies to GST notices.

    What documents are needed to file an income tax return?

    Usually PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16 or salary slips, Form 26AS / AIS, bank statements and investment proofs. See the Document Checklist above for the full list, or message us and we will tell you exactly what applies to you.

    Do you offer online consultation and document sharing?

    Yes. You can speak to us on phone or WhatsApp and share documents digitally, so you don't need to visit the office for most services.

    How long does company registration take?

    It depends on how quickly documents are ready and on government processing. Contact us with your requirement and we will give you a realistic timeline.

    Is my financial information kept confidential?

    Yes. Your documents and details are used only for the work you have asked us to do and are not shared with anyone else.
